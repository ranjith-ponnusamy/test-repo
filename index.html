About

My journey started in fields, not spreadsheets. During my B.Sc. in Agriculture at Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU), I became fascinated by how small decisions in production, procurement, and pricing could reshape outcomes for farmers and businesses alike. That curiosity led me to the Imperial School of Agri-Business, where I completed my MBA in Food & Agribusiness Management and discovered the power of analytics in strategic decision-making.

What began as a domain-first perspective evolved into a passion for business intelligence. I transitioned into analytics by learning to work deeply with data profiling, KPI reporting, dashboard development, and stakeholder communication. Today, I combine agricultural domain knowledge with modern analytics tools to tell clear, practical stories from complex datasets. Whether I am analyzing transaction-level sales or identifying performance gaps across locations, my focus remains the same: translate numbers into decisions people can act on.

Looking ahead, I am excited to build data-driven solutions that help organizations grow with more confidence, clarity, and measurable impact.
